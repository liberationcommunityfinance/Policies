# Privacy Policy for Liberation Community Finance

**Last Updated:** 18th October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Liberation Community Finance** ("Liberation," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you use our mobile application, USSD service, and related digital banking services (the "Services"). We are committed to handling your personal data responsibly and in line with the **Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 of Uganda** and the regulations made under it. By using our Services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use our Services:

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Identity & KYC Information:** When you register or are verified, you may provide your full name, date of birth, gender, national identification number (NIN) or other ID, and a photo/selfie and ID images where required to open and verify an account.
- **Contact Information:** Your phone number(s), email address, and physical or postal address.
- **Membership & Financial Information:** Your member number, account numbers, savings and loan balances, transaction history, loan applications, and the financial details needed to assess and service them.
- **Payment Details:** The mobile-money numbers and bank account details you enter to make deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and bill payments.
- **Security Credentials:** Your service PIN and security-question answers (stored in protected form).

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, app version, and mobile network information.
- **Usage & Diagnostic Data:** How you interact with the Services, feature usage, and crash and performance logs that help us keep the Services reliable and secure.
- **Location Information:** With your consent, approximate or precise location to support certain features and to help prevent fraud. You can disable this in your device settings.

### 1.3 Biometric Data
If you enable fingerprint or biometric sign-in, the check is performed by your device's own secure hardware. **Your biometric data stays on your device and is never transmitted to or stored by Liberation.**

### 1.4 Third-Party Information
- **Payment & Mobile-Money Partners:** Transaction status and confirmations from our payment aggregator (PegPay / Pegasus Technologies), mobile network operators, and partner banks when you move money.
- **Google Play Services & Firebase:** On Android, these provide push notifications, crash reporting, and basic analytics, and may collect device identifiers as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Open, operate, and secure your membership and accounts.
- Process deposits, withdrawals, transfers, bill payments, and airtime, and send you transaction confirmations and receipts.
- Receive, assess, approve, and service loan applications, including credit assessment.
- Verify your identity and meet our Know-Your-Customer (KYC), Anti-Money-Laundering (AML), and Counter-Financing-of-Terrorism (CFT) obligations.
- Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, and protect the security of your account and our systems.
- Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
- Send you service and transactional messages, and — where you have not opted out — relevant product information.
- Improve and develop our Services and comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

---

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do **not** sell your personal data. We share it only as needed to provide the Services or as required by law, including with:
- **Payment & Settlement Partners** (PegPay / Pegasus Technologies, mobile network operators, and partner banks) to complete the transactions you request.
- **Service Providers** who help us operate the Services (e.g., hosting, notifications, analytics, customer support), under confidentiality obligations.
- **Credit Reference Bureaus and partners** in connection with loans, where applicable and permitted by law.
- **Regulators, auditors, courts, and law enforcement** where required by law or to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.
- **Other parties with your consent.**

---

## 4. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit, PIN and biometric protection, access controls, and monitoring. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so while we work hard to protect your data we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your PIN and device secure, and tell us immediately if you suspect unauthorised access.

---

## 5. Data Retention

We keep your personal data for as long as you hold a membership or account with us, and afterwards for the period required to meet our legal, regulatory, accounting, and record-keeping obligations (for example, AML record-keeping). When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

---

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you and request a copy.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Delete** your data where we are not required to keep it by law.
- **Object to or restrict** certain processing, and **withdraw consent** you previously gave.
- **Opt out** of marketing messages at any time.
- **Lodge a complaint** with the Personal Data Protection Office of Uganda.

To exercise these rights, contact us at **info@liberation.co.ug**. Some data is necessary to provide regulated financial services; if you ask us to delete it while you remain a member, we may be unable to continue providing the Services.

---

## 7. Data Location

Your data is processed and stored on systems operated for Liberation Community Finance. Where any processing takes place outside Uganda (for example, through cloud or notification providers), we take steps to ensure your data continues to be protected in line with this policy and applicable law.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are intended for members aged 18 and above. Accounts for minors are opened and operated only through a parent or guardian in line with our membership rules. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children; if we learn that we have, we will delete it.

---

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will post the updated policy here and update the "Last Updated" date, and where appropriate notify you in the app. Your continued use of the Services after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
